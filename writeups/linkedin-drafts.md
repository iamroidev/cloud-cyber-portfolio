## Draft B: Zero Trust paper review (post a few days later)

I reviewed a TechRxiv paper on Zero Trust adoption in Sub-Saharan Africa.

The point that stuck with me: awareness is rising, but skills, cost, and weak local vendor ecosystems still block real deployments. Zero Trust is not just a product purchase. It is identity, least privilege, monitoring, and response habits.

What I am taking into my own work:

- treat continuous verification as something I practice in projects, not only as a buzzword
- keep building applied case studies (risk assessment, log analysis, incident write-ups)
- adapt security designs to local constraints instead of copying mature-market playbooks wholesale

Paper:
https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.176617696.66291663/v1
